Admission guides

The steps scammers hide behind. We don't quote fees or cut-off marks here, because they change each session. Each guide tells you where to confirm the current figure.

Accepting your admission on JAMB CAPS
  1. Log in to the JAMB CAPS portal and check your admission status.
  2. If it says admitted, accept it. Do this promptly. Don't wait for days.
  3. Print your admission letter from JAMB.
  4. Pay UNN's acceptance fee on the official UNN portal only, and keep the receipt.

Fees differ by admission list and session. Read our acceptance fee guide.

How Post-UTME and aggregate scores work

UNN combines your JAMB score with your Post-UTME or screening result into one aggregate. The weighting and cut-offs are set each session.

  • Read UNN's official admission notice for this session's formula.
  • Compare against last session's departmental cut-offs to set realistic choices.
  • Ask in your faculty group, then verify any number on unn.edu.ng.
Documents to bring for clearance
  • JAMB result and admission letter
  • O'Level result(s): WAEC, NECO or NABTEB
  • Birth certificate and LGA/state of origin proof
  • Passport photographs
  • Acceptance fee receipt and Post-UTME printout

The exact checklist is on the UNN portal. Make copies of everything and arrive early.

Direct Entry (200 level)

DE candidates apply through JAMB, then complete UNN's DE screening. Accepted qualifications include ND, HND, NCE, IJMB, JUPEB and a first degree, subject to department requirements. Request your transcript early because it can take weeks.

Five signs it's a scam

Money for a "slot"

UNN admission comes only through JAMB CAPS. Nobody can sell you a place.

Score "upgrades"

No agent can change a JAMB result.

Personal account numbers

Official fees go through UNN's portal. A friend's Opay or PalmPay is a red flag.

Look-alike websites

Type unn.edu.ng yourself. Don't trust links sent in DMs.

"Come with cash"

Anyone asking you to meet and bring money for admission help is not helping you.

How to accept your UNN admission and pay the acceptance fee

Published 29 October 2026. 3 minute read.

Seen "Admitted" on JAMB CAPS? Do these four things in order. Skipping the first one blocks everything after it.

  1. Accept your admission on JAMB CAPS. Click "Accept Admission" on your dashboard straight away.
  2. Generate your payment invoice. Log in to the UNN portal with your JAMB registration number and generate a Remita invoice.
  3. Pay the acceptance fee using that invoice only.
  4. Complete registration. Print your admission slip and acceptance form with your invoice and confirmation numbers.

How much is the fee?

Be careful here. A guide circulating on Instagram says ₦50,000 for non-regulated programmes and ₦100,000 for regulated ones (Medicine, Law, Pharmacy, Nursing and Dentistry). We couldn't match those figures to an official UNN notice. Reports of UNN's 2025/26 admission notices show ₦30,000 for some lists and ₦50,000 for others, and we found no confirmed 2026/27 amount on the day we published.

The rule that protects you: pay only the amount on the Remita invoice you generated yourself on the official UNN portal. If a person gives you an amount, an account number or a link, it's a scam, even if the number sounds right.

Fees change by session and admission list. Confirm on unn.edu.ng before paying, and ask in the Announcements group if anything looks different.

For parents and guardians

Do you charge any fees?
No. Joining, guides and group help are free. If someone uses our name to ask for money, it isn't us.
Can you guarantee admission?
No. Nobody can. Admission is decided by UNN through JAMB CAPS. We help candidates understand the process and avoid scams.
Who runs the groups?
Current UNN students, coordinated by the founder. Acadites is not part of the University's administration.
How do I report a suspicious message?
Screenshot it and send it to us on WhatsApp or email. Report fake portals to JAMB or UNN directly.
